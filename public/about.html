Quick answers

Mukunda in short.

What is Mukunda Technologies?

Mukunda Technologies is a South African software company. We build our own software products, starting with Caterflow, and we build custom software, websites and automation for businesses.

What does Mukunda build?

Our live product is Caterflow, a white-label catering platform. BizOps (business management software) and My Roots (a family history product) are in development, and OneProfile (a consent-led onboarding service) is in a partner-first pilot. We also build custom software for clients.

Where is Mukunda based?

Mukunda Technologies is based in South Africa and works with South African businesses and organisations.
