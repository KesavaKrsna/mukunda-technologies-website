Caterflow

A white-label catering platform. Caterers get their own branded online storefront for menus and home-meal orders, plus quotes, customer management and a QR menu, all in one place.

Available now for South African caterers and restaurants. Plans and pricing are on the Caterflow website.

OneProfile

A consent-led onboarding service. People build a profile and document collection once, then share it with an institution only with their explicit, scoped consent, so that institutions can make digital onboarding easier to complete.

Not publicly available. We are working with partner institutions first. Institutions that would like to discuss a pilot are welcome to get in touch.
